About Asamarix

Asamarix Technologies is an independent cybersecurity and IT consultancy built around the work of testing, securing, and operating business-critical systems.

What Sets Asamarix Apart

Security advice grounded in how systems run

We consider who owns each system, what it connects to, and when it can be changed. Those details shape the remediation order, especially in environments where a failed integration can interrupt daily operations.

Experience across testing, implementation, and handover

Our work draws on enterprise systems delivery as well as security assessment. We can help translate findings into implementation tasks, define how to verify the fix, and document what the operations team needs after release.

Direct access to technical leadership

Engagements are founder-led. Scope, constraints, and technical decisions are discussed with the person responsible for the work.

Marino Ozkaya

Founder and Chief Information Security Officer

Marino Ozkaya is the founder and CISO of Asamarix Technologies and currently works as a red team operator at Vault Breach. His cybersecurity work focuses on penetration testing, vulnerability assessment, and understanding how weaknesses in applications, networks, and identity systems can combine to create larger risks. His hands-on work spans web application security testing, network reconnaissance, packet analysis, and Active Directory attack-path analysis. He brings a defender’s perspective to that work, examining what a weakness exposes, where existing controls can limit its impact, and which fixes should come first.

Marino’s security work builds on years of delivering technology projects in Asia for multinational organizations. At DSV Solutions, he led digital solutions initiatives across warehouse management systems, carrier integrations, and business automation. Working in a global environment, he coordinated regional technical teams, local operations, and client stakeholders through testing and production rollouts. That experience gives him a practical understanding of how enterprise systems are connected, where trust boundaries can break down, and what it takes to implement security fixes in environments that need to stay operational.
