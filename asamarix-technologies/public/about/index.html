About Asamarix

Asamarix Technologies is an independent cybersecurity and IT consultancy built around the work of testing, securing, and operating business-critical systems.

What sets Asamarix apart

Security advice grounded in how systems run

We consider who owns each system, what it connects to, and when it can be changed. Those details shape the remediation order, especially in environments where a failed integration can interrupt daily operations.

Experience across testing, implementation, and handover

Our work draws on enterprise systems delivery as well as security assessment. We can help translate findings into implementation tasks, define how to verify the fix, and document what the operations team needs after release.

Direct access to technical leadership

Engagements are founder-led. Scope, constraints, and technical decisions are discussed with the person responsible for the work.

Marino Ozkaya

Founder and Chief Information Security Officer

Marino’s background spans cybersecurity, enterprise systems implementation, and technical project delivery. Before founding Asamarix, he led digital solutions work in logistics, coordinating warehouse management system implementations, carrier integrations, testing, and production rollouts.

His experience with API and EDI interfaces, workflow automation, and operational handovers informs how Asamarix scopes engagements and communicates technical findings.
