Illustrative deliverable

Sample penetration test report

An excerpt from a fictional assessment of a shipment operations portal, using synthetic data. The example shows the level of detail an operations team needs to act on a finding.

Executive summary

The example assessment identified a gap in how the portal enforced multifactor authentication for privileged users. A compromised operator password could have exposed shipment records or let someone change shipment statuses. We would prioritize enforcing MFA for all privileged users and confirming that the emergency access process is monitored.

Scope and limitations

The exercise covered a staging portal with synthetic shipments and two roles, read-only dispatcher and operations administrator. The example excludes production, carrier systems and third-party identity infrastructure.

Finding

Multifactor authentication was optional for operations administrators

Example severity
High
Affected area
Operations administrator sign-in
Suggested owner
Identity and access management, with the portal owner

Evidence summary. The assessment record showed a privileged test account could sign in with a password alone. The portal audit event confirmed that the session was granted administrator access.

Business impact. An unauthorized change to a shipment status could trigger downstream exceptions, delay fulfillment, and require manual reconciliation.

Recommended action. Audit privileged accounts, require MFA through the identity policy, and document an approved emergency-access procedure with monitoring. Schedule the change with operations and brief staff on recovery before enforcement.

Retest criteria

  • Every in-scope privileged account is subject to MFA policy.
  • Administrator sessions are granted only after the required factor is satisfied.
  • Routine dispatcher access still works and emergency access produces a reviewable alert.
