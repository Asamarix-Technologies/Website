Penetration Testing

Assess agreed web applications, APIs, or network assets to understand which security weaknesses could expose sensitive data or business functions. Engagements begin with written authorization, an agreed asset list, testing windows, and escalation contacts.

Typical deliverables. An executive summary, prioritized technical findings with evidence and remediation advice, and a walkthrough with system owners. Retesting can be scoped for fixes that need validation.

Defensive Cybersecurity

Review the controls your team uses to prevent, detect, and respond to incidents. Work can cover identity and access, configuration baselines, patching, logging coverage, and incident procedures.

Typical deliverables. A prioritized remediation plan, configuration guidance, and runbook updates your team can use during incidents or routine maintenance.

Systems Administration

Help maintain reliable Windows and Linux environments through account and permission reviews, patch planning, service configuration, and backup and recovery checks.

Typical deliverables. An inventory of the systems in scope, agreed maintenance tasks, documented changes, and a handover with verification and recovery steps.

IT Project Management

Coordinate implementations across business owners, vendors, and technical teams. We help turn requirements into milestones, track dependencies, and plan integration testing, user acceptance testing, and cutover.

Typical deliverables. A project plan, risk and decision tracking, launch readiness criteria, and a transition plan for production support.

Integration and Automation

Connect warehouse systems, carrier platforms, and business tools through API or EDI integrations. For recurring manual work, we map inputs, approvals, and exceptions before designing an automation.

Typical deliverables. Interface mappings, test scenarios, exception handling, and operating instructions for the people who will maintain the workflow.
